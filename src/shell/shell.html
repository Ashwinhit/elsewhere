HOW THE MATCH WORKS

1

Profile. Every Earth place gets a score on 16 terrain features, such as dryness, cold, ruggedness, ice, basalt and dunes.

2

Compare. The profile is compared with 30 Moon and Mars sites: bases, past landings and proposed future landing zones.

3

Scan. Each country is also scanned on a grid, so new candidate spots turn up even where no landform has a name.
